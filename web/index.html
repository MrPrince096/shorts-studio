Story Studio

Long-form narration videos — 3 to 10 minutes
Long videos take a while — a 3-min story ≈ 5–8 min to make. Uses your Quick-settings voice, visuals & AI (works offline with Gemma + Piper).

Song Studio

Lyric videos — write lyrics from a topic (or bring your own song), with a relevant image per line
Note: this makes an instrumental lyric video (music + on-screen lyrics + a relevant image per line) — not AI singing. Bring your own song for vocals. Set Aspect → 1:1 in Song settings for the classic look.

Miniature World

Tiny tilt-shift diorama videos — little figurine people + oversized objects, cinematic macro. From a topic.
The tilt-shift look + story are done locally. For true motion like the viral clips, Cloud (Kling/Seedance) matches best (needs a fal.ai key); Local LTX is free but softer and slow (~5–8 min per scene on an M1). Renders vertical.

My Clips → Short

Bring your own videos & photos — e.g. Veo clips from Google Flow — and stitch them into a finished short with captions, music & voice.
Tip: make cinematic clips in Google Flow (included in your Google AI Pro plan), download them, and drop them here to add captions/voice/music. Clips play in the order you add them; add a caption per item below.

Dub & Subtitle

Upload any video → transcribe → translate → new AI voiceover + subtitles. Any language → any language.
Runs locally & free. It replaces the audio with the new voice (mouths won't lip-sync) and uses one voice for everyone. Best on short clips — long videos take a while. Transcription runs on your Mac (first run downloads a small model).

Autopilot

A self-running channel — it invents topics and generates videos on a timer, into your library
Off
Not scheduled
Autopilot runs while the app is open. It files videos into My Videos for you to review — it never posts anywhere on its own. Uses free cloud images by default (turn on Local engines to keep it fully offline).

Comparison Studio

Split-screen videos like "Textbook vs Casual" — two AI characters, native + romaji + English captions
Static mode is fast (~1–2 min) and fully local-capable. Animate matches the reference's motion but is heavy — it makes 2 AI clips per scene, so keep scenes low and expect a long wait on an M1. Renders vertical 720×1280.

Ads Studio

UGC, product & social ads from one image
Upload a product/person image → AI reads it and pitches ad ideas. Pick one and it renders a UGC-style ad: your product footage + voiceover + captions. Talking-face creators need an avatar API (see My Creators).
Presets tune tone & pacing per format. Aspect ratio comes from Quick settings (9:16, 1:1, 16:9…).
